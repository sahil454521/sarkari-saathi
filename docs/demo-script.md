# 60-second demo video

## Set up

Use two phones. Phone A runs the app. Phone B films over the shoulder in portrait (9:16), so the shot shows the screen, the person speaking, and both voices. Android won't let its screen recorder use the microphone while Chrome is listening, so a screen recording alone would miss the speaker's voice.

If you want sharp close-ups of the form filling in, also screen-record Phone A with "Media sounds" only and use those clips as inserts.

Before you press record:

- Open https://sarkari-saathi-kjgb.onrender.com (without `?debug=1`) a minute early: the free server takes up to a minute to wake up.
- Do one full practice run. It wakes the server and caches the greeting.
- Quiet room, phone volume high, Do Not Disturb on, brightness up, no notifications on screen.
- "Sunita" is an invented persona. Cast someone who speaks Marathi naturally and agrees to appear. Use only the invented details below: no real names, numbers, Aadhaar or documents.

## What Sunita says

The conversation is hands-free: after each question the mic pulses violet and listens on its own. She can start answering as soon as it pulses, and a pause of up to 2 seconds is fine. Each reply comes back in about 3 seconds.

| App asks | Sunita answers | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| Greeting: tell me your age, work and where you live | मी पंचेचाळीस वर्षांची शेतकरी बाई आहे, नाशिकला राहते. | I'm a 45-year-old woman farmer, I live in Nashik. |
| What colour is your ration card? | पिवळं रेशन कार्ड आहे, दोन वर्षांची मुलगी आहे, घरचं वर्षाचं उत्पन्न नव्वद हजार आहे. | Yellow ration card, a two-year-old daughter, family income ninety thousand a year. |
| Do you pay income tax? | नाही, मी आयकर भरत नाही. | No, I don't pay income tax. |
| Is the farmland in your name? | हो, जमीन माझ्या नावावर आहे. | Yes, the land is in my name. |
| Do you have an LPG connection? | हो, गॅस आहे. | Yes, we have gas. |
| Are you pregnant right now? | नाही. | No. |

The results then show "तुम्ही 8 योजनांसाठी पात्र असू शकता": "पात्र" (eligible) on Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Jan Arogya and Sukanya Samriddhi, "संभाव्य" (likely) on PM-KISAN, Namo Shetkari, crop insurance, PM-JAY, Ladki Bahin and Lek Ladki. This flow was checked against the live code on 2026-10-20.
